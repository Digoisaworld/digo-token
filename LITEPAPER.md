# DIGO Litepaper — Working Draft

**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Working draft. The DIGO SPL token has been minted on Solana Mainnet; proposed ecosystem products are not live.

## 1. Vision
DIGO aims to build a technology-focused ecosystem at the intersection of Solana and artificial intelligence. The long-term direction is practical infrastructure for AI agents, developers and digital services — not a charity or donation project.

## 2. The opportunity
AI agents increasingly need reliable ways to discover services, access APIs, use compute and pay for digital resources. DIGO will explore transparent, blockchain-based coordination and settlement, while keeping model inference and other compute-heavy workloads off-chain.

## 3. Proposed technology ecosystem
All items below are concepts until shipped and independently testable:
- **DIGO Agent Hub:** directory of AI agents and tools, with service descriptions, versions and reputation signals.
- **AI API Marketplace:** discovery and metered access to participating model, data and automation APIs.
- **Agent-to-Agent Payments:** payment and settlement flows for agents purchasing digital services.
- **Compute Access:** integrations with independent GPU / inference providers; DIGO would not itself claim to operate a GPU network unless it actually does.
- **Developer SDK:** documented interfaces, example integrations and test environment.
- **Transparency Dashboard:** token supply, treasury, allocations, governance actions and ecosystem usage metrics.

## 4. Potential token utility
Subject to technical, legal and security review, DIGO may be used for service payments, API access credits, agent settlement, developer incentives and ecosystem governance. Any payment or access utility must have clear pricing, refund/error handling, anti-abuse controls and a usable non-token fallback where legally or operationally appropriate. No utility is live today and no future demand is guaranteed.

## 5. Token design
Proposed DIGO token on Solana, 8 decimals, with an initial mint target of **100 billion DIGO**. The founder's current preference is to retain mint authority for possible future issuance and revoke freeze authority. Consequently, 100 billion is not a permanent supply cap. Future issuance policy, authority custody and allocation/vesting details must be published before launch.

## 6. Architecture principles
- Keep AI inference and compute off-chain; use Solana for settlement, identity references and verifiable coordination where appropriate.
- Minimize on-chain personal data; never put private prompts, API keys or user secrets on-chain.
- Use audited or established programs where possible; custom programs require independent review.
- Publish service fees, treasury wallets, supply changes and material governance decisions.
- Do not claim decentralization, AI performance or provider partnerships without evidence.

## 7. Delivery stages
1. Foundation: token policy, branding, legal review and public disclosures.
2. Developer prototype: Agent Hub and testnet payment flow.
3. AI service integrations: limited pilot with named providers and measurable service-level data.
4. Mainnet utility: only after threat modeling, audits, operational controls and user testing.
5. Ecosystem and market readiness: independent usage evidence, liquidity transparency and exchange due diligence.

## 8. Risks
DIGO may fail to gain adoption, lose market value, face liquidity constraints, suffer software or custody incidents, encounter AI service outages or be affected by regulatory changes. Active mint authority creates dilution risk. Holding DIGO does not guarantee profits, redemption, governance rights or access to services unless separately documented.

## 9. No guarantees
No return, price, liquidity, listing, partnership, provider capacity or future utility is guaranteed. This draft is informational, not an offer to sell securities or financial advice.
