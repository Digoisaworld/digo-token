# DIGO Litepaper — Working Draft

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Pre-launch. DIGO has not yet been minted on Solana Mainnet.

## 1. Vision
DIGO aims to become a community-first Solana ecosystem that turns kindness into measurable, transparent participation. It is intended as a long-term product project, not a short-lived token promotion.

## 2. The problem
Communities need transparent ways to coordinate contributions, fund public-benefit campaigns and show what happened after funds were collected. Token projects too often claim utility before products exist. DIGO will build in stages and publish evidence of delivery.

## 3. Proposed ecosystem
- **DIGO Community:** public proposals, discussion, decisions and progress tracking.
- **Kind Actions:** animal-welfare and local kindness campaigns. Each campaign must disclose its organizer, budget, beneficiary checks, delivery evidence and close-out report.
- **DIGO Rewards:** possible rewards for verifiable contributions, only after anti-sybil controls, eligibility rules, budget limits and abuse handling are tested. No guaranteed yield.
- **DIGO Pay:** pilot voluntary payments and donations using Solana-compatible payment flows.
- **Public dashboard:** mint supply, circulating-supply methodology, treasury wallets, liquidity and delivery metrics.

## 4. Token design
Proposed DIGO token on Solana, 9 decimals, initial mint target one billion units. The founder currently prefers retaining mint authority for future issuance and revoking freeze authority. Therefore DIGO must not be described as fixed-supply or permanently capped while mint authority remains active. Final allocations, authority custody and issuance governance must be approved before launch.

## 5. Governance and treasury
Intended direction: transparent treasury, multisig custody, published signer policy, public wallet addresses and regular reports. Governance rules must be specified before binding votes are launched.

## 6. Delivery
Foundation and disclosures; transparency and security; utility MVP; ecosystem integrations; then exchange-readiness review. A feature will be called live only after it is deployed and independently testable.

## 7. Risks
DIGO may fail to gain adoption, lose market value, face liquidity constraints, suffer software or custody incidents, or be affected by regulatory changes. Ownership does not guarantee profits, redemption, governance rights or access to services unless separately documented.

## 8. No guarantees
No return, price, liquidity, listing, partnership, donation outcome or future utility is guaranteed. This draft is informational, not an offer to sell securities or financial advice.
