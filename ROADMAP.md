# DIGO Roadmap

This roadmap is conditional on delivery, security, community demand and legal review. Dates will be added only when resourced.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- Confirm token control policy and publish responsible team/contact details.
- Publish logo, metadata JSON and canonical links.
- Publish mint address only after mainnet verification.
- Publish allocation table, vesting contracts/escrows and treasury wallet.
- Create a public issue tracker and monthly transparency reports.

## Phase 1 — Trust and transparency
- Public dashboard: total supply, circulating supply methodology, holders, treasury and liquidity.
- Multisig treasury with a published signer policy and emergency procedure.
- Independent review of token setup, website and any custom programs.
- Reproducible deployment instructions and verified source.

## Phase 2 — Utility MVP
- DIGO Community Hub: proposals, voting snapshots and published outcomes.
- Kind Actions: transparent animal-welfare/community campaigns with evidence of delivery.
- Solana Pay pilot for donations and participating merchants.
- Contribution rewards only after anti-sybil, budget and abuse controls are tested.

## Phase 3 — Ecosystem
- Integrations with wallets, explorers, payment providers and analytics.
- Merchant/creator tools and documented APIs.
- Community grants with public applications, decisions and reporting.

## Phase 4 — Market readiness
- Legal and jurisdictional review.
- Security assessment and incident-response plan.
- Liquidity ownership, lock/vesting evidence and market-maker disclosures where applicable.
- Complete exchange due-diligence package: legal entity/contact, token contract/mint, supply, holders, audits, liquidity, trading history, risks and compliance materials.

**No exchange listing is promised.** Exchanges make independent decisions and may require additional criteria.
