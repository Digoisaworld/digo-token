# Exchange Readiness Dossier

Working due-diligence checklist only. This is not a claim that DIGO is approved or eligible for any exchange.

## Project identity and technology
- [ ] Responsible legal entity/operator, jurisdiction and public contact documented
- [ ] Official domain, team, litepaper, roadmap and community channels verified
- [ ] AI product claims supported by a working demo, named providers and measurable usage
- [ ] Clear distinction between live services, pilots and roadmap concepts

## On-chain asset
- [ ] Mainnet mint address independently verified
- [ ] Token program, metadata, name, symbol, decimals and logo verified
- [ ] Initial mint target (100 billion) and actual minted supply verified
- [ ] Active mint authority, custody and future issuance policy disclosed
- [ ] Freeze authority state verified
- [ ] Allocation wallets, vesting, treasury and holder concentration disclosed
- [ ] Circulating-supply methodology and dilution risks disclosed

## Security and operations
- [ ] Multisig/key management policy documented
- [ ] Independent review of custom programs, website and payment integrations completed
- [ ] AI/API provider security, privacy and service continuity assessed
- [ ] Incident-response contact and procedure published
- [ ] Dependency and secret scans pass

## Market integrity and legal
- [ ] Organic community and product usage evidenced
- [ ] Liquidity pools, LP control/lock status and funding disclosed
- [ ] No wash trading, fabricated volume or undisclosed paid promotion
- [ ] Qualified legal review for relevant jurisdictions
- [ ] AML/sanctions and AI/data privacy obligations assessed where applicable

## Submission package
Prepare dated materials covering official contacts, mint/explorer links, tokenomics, authority state, audits, liquidity, legal materials, AI product demonstrations and usage metrics. Every exchange makes its own decision and may request additional evidence. No listing is guaranteed.
