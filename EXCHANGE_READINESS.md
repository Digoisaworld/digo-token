# Exchange Readiness Dossier

Working due-diligence checklist only. This is not a claim that DIGO is approved or eligible for any exchange.

## Project identity
- [ ] Responsible legal entity/operator and jurisdiction documented
- [ ] Official domain ownership and public contact verified
- [ ] Team, litepaper, roadmap and community channels published

## On-chain asset
- [ ] Mainnet mint address independently verified
- [ ] Token program, metadata program, name, symbol, decimals and image verified
- [ ] Initial supply and authority state independently checked
- [ ] Mint authority custody and future issuance policy disclosed
- [ ] Freeze authority state verified
- [ ] Allocation wallets, vesting and treasury addresses published
- [ ] Holder concentration and circulating-supply methodology disclosed

## Security
- [ ] Multisig/key management policy documented
- [ ] Independent review of custom programs and website completed
- [ ] Incident-response contact and procedure published
- [ ] Dependency and secret scans pass

## Market integrity
- [ ] Organic community and product usage evidenced
- [ ] Liquidity pools, LP control/lock status and funding disclosed
- [ ] No wash trading, fabricated volume or undisclosed paid promotion
- [ ] Market-maker arrangements and compensation disclosed

## Legal and compliance
- [ ] Qualified legal review for relevant jurisdictions
- [ ] AML/sanctions obligations assessed where applicable
- [ ] Rewards, donations and marketing mechanics reviewed
- [ ] Terms and privacy policy published for user-facing services

## Submission package
Prepare a dated package with official contacts, mint and explorer links, tokenomics, authority state, audit reports, liquidity details, legal materials and product metrics. Every exchange makes its own decision and may request additional evidence. No listing is guaranteed.
